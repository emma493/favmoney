18 U.S.C. § 2257 Record-Keeping Requirements Compliance Statement

All visual content displayed on Favmoney (favmoney.site) depicts individuals who were at least eighteen (18) years of age at the time of creation. Favmoney complies with the record-keeping requirements of 18 U.S.C. § 2257 and 28 C.F.R. § 75.

The custodian of records for Favmoney maintains all required documentation, including proof of age and government-issued identification for every performer depicted, and makes such records available for inspection as required by law.

Favmoney aggregates and links to third-party hosted content and does not produce content itself. All third-party content providers are required to certify compliance with 18 U.S.C. § 2257 before their content is linked.
